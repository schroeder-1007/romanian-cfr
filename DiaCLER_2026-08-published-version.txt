$ DiaCLER: A debugged implementation of the ordered chanɡes of Pardess (1990) as a DiaSim cascade
$
$ The ɡoal of this debugged cascade is to present the most complete and accurate list of the sound changes that occurred from Latin to Daco-Romanian 
$ while being as faithful as possible to Pardess' original description.
$
$ When a formal rule is not well-suited for computerized application, we chanɡe it so that it works in DiaSim while
$ maintaininɡ Pardess' intentions accordinɡ to his rule title. This also applies for cases where Pardess made a 
$ minor mistake in the formal rule but his rule title and example lexica suɡɡest his intentions.
$
$ Staɡes are included when a new chapter is reached in Pardess' rule list: ~Balkan Romance (R25), ~Common Romanian (R45)



$ Latin [(ˈ)ae̯] simplification rules.
	$ [(ˈ)ae̯] simplifies when between word boundary and sonorant (vowels count).
		e̯ > ∅ / # [+lo] __ [+son]
	$ [(ˈ)ae̯] simplifies when between a labial consonant (w/ optional [r] in cluster) and a coronal.
		e̯ > ∅ / [+lab,+cons] (r) [+lo] __ [+cor]

$ High vowel desyllabification after [(ˈ)u].
	$ Bleeds Pope's yod and w formation rule.
	$ Affects viduum, astruica(re), and amendui.
		[+syl,-long,-stres,+hi] > [+hi,-syl,+tense] / [+syl,+hi,+back] __

$ <de> proclisis.
	$ The <de> vowel appears to delete the following vowel and assume its stress.
		[+syl,+long] [+syl,-long,αprim,βstres] > [αprim,βstres] ∅

$ NEW RULE: #s'VCV > #sC'V (super > sper)
	$ Labialism of first vowel and consonant have to match.
	$ Frontness of first vowel and consonant also have to match.
	$ Syncopated vowel must be short.
		[+prim,αround,βfront,-long] [-cont,αlab,βfront] [+syl] > ∅ [-cont] [+prim] / # s __
			$ This only affects super>spre.

$ NEW RULE: cōn- stressing.
	$ Seems to be motivated by a confusion of which round vowel gets primary stress.
		[+long,+round,-hi] > [+prim] / # k __ n ([-syl])* [+round,+prim]
		[+prim] > [-stres] / [+prim] ([-syl])* __
